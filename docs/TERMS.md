# Ticket and membership terms

This is the society's terms and conditions for tickets and membership, kept here
so that they survive handover and have an edit history.

**The live copy is in TryBooking**, not on this website. It lives in
**Dashboard → Global event settings → Event terms and conditions**, and applies
account-wide to every event, not just membership.

> **This is not legal advice.** It is a reasonable, proportionate starting point
> written for a student society. Read it before using it, and if the society ever
> takes significant sums or runs events with real risk attached, get it looked at
> properly.

---

## Keeping this file and TryBooking in step

There are two copies, which means they can drift. The rule:

**Edit this file first, then paste the whole thing into TryBooking.** Do not edit
the TryBooking field directly, or the next person will find two versions and no
way to tell which is current.

The version that legally applies to a booking is the one shown to the buyer at
the time they booked — so if these change mid-year, previous bookings keep the
older terms. That is what clause 8 says, and it is why the history in Git is
worth having.

## The terms

```
Cambridge University Indian Classical Music Society — Ticket and Membership Terms

1. WHO WE ARE
These terms apply to tickets and memberships bought from the Cambridge
University Indian Classical Music Society ("the Society") through TryBooking.
Questions: indianclassicalmusic@cambridgesu.co.uk.

2. MEMBERSHIP
2.1 Society membership 2026–27 is valid from 08:00 on Tuesday 15 September 2026
    until 16:00 on Saturday 30 October 2027 (UK time).
2.2 Membership entitles the holder to:
    (a) take part as a performer in Society concerts, subject to the programme
        for each event and to the Committee's decisions about who performs;
    (b) vote at any Annual General Meeting of the Society held during the
        membership period; and
    (c) stand for election to the Committee at any such Annual General Meeting.
    Membership fees also support the Society's concerts, workshops and other
    activities.
2.3 The rights in 2.2(b) and 2.2(c) are subject to the Society's constitution
    and to the rules of Cambridge SU, which take precedence over these terms.
2.4 Membership is personal to the named member and is not transferable. Each
    membership covers one named person, and only that person may perform, vote
    or stand under it.
2.5 We ask for the member's name when you book. Please give the name of the
    person the membership is for, not the person paying — we use it to check
    membership at events.

3. TICKETS AND ENTRY
3.1 Please bring your ticket, on your phone or printed. Adding it to Apple
    Wallet or Google Wallet when you book is the most reliable way to keep it.
3.2 We may check the name on your ticket at the door.
3.3 Tickets are not transferable and may not be resold.

4. REFUNDS AND CANCELLATIONS
4.1 If we cancel an event, you will receive a full refund.
4.2 If we postpone an event, your ticket stays valid for the new date. If you
    cannot make it, contact us and we will refund you.
4.3 We do not otherwise refund tickets to individual events, because we commit
    to venue and artist costs in advance.
4.4 Memberships may be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase, provided
    you have not already performed at or attended an event as a member.

5. CHANGES TO EVENTS
Programmes, performers and venues are occasionally subject to change. Where a
change is significant we will email ticket holders.

6. YOUR INFORMATION
6.1 Bookings are processed by TryBooking, who take your payment and hold your
    booking details. We receive your name, email address and booking details.
6.2 We use these only to administer your booking and membership and to contact
    you about what you booked. We do not sell your details or pass them to
    anyone else.
6.3 To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email
    indianclassicalmusic@cambridgesu.co.uk.

7. LIABILITY
Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused
by our negligence, or for fraud.

8. CHANGES TO THESE TERMS
We may update these terms. The version that applies is the one shown when you
book.
```

## Why some clauses are worded the way they are

Recorded so a future committee changes them deliberately rather than by accident.

**2.2(a) does not promise a performance.** "Entitles the holder to perform" would
be a commitment the society cannot keep — there are a handful of concerts a year
and no way to programme every member. Someone who paid and was not programmed
could fairly say they did not get what they bought. The wording keeps the benefit
real while leaving the committee the discretion it actually needs.

**2.2(b) and (c) say "any AGM held during the membership period".** The
membership year ends in October, so somebody joining late could otherwise buy a
membership whose AGM has already happened and feel short-changed.

**2.3 makes the constitution win.** Voting and standing for committee are
governed by the society's constitution and by Cambridge SU, which may impose
conditions these terms do not mention — for instance requiring that candidates be
current members of the University. A ticketing term should never quietly override
the constitution.

**2.4 matters more than it looks** now that membership carries a vote.
Non-transferability stops somebody buying several memberships and distributing
voting rights.

**3.3 exists because there is nothing to switch off.** TryBooking has no
buyer-facing ticket transfer feature, so there is no setting that disables
transfer. Stating it in the terms is the only place it can live.

**4.1 depends on a TryBooking setting.** "A full refund" is only true because the
society absorbs TryBooking's 15p ticket fee, which is **never refunded** to
anybody. Since the buyer never pays it, they get back exactly what they paid. If a
future committee moves that fee onto the buyer, this clause quietly becomes a
promise that cannot be kept — the buyer would be 15p short on every refund,
including ones caused by the society cancelling. Either leave the fee setting
alone or reword this clause to "a full refund of the ticket price". See
[TICKETING.md](TICKETING.md), "Before you sell".

**4.4 gives 14 days voluntarily.** UK distance-selling rules normally grant a
14-day cooling-off right, but exempt leisure services supplied on a specific
date — which plainly covers a concert ticket and much less plainly covers a
13-month membership. Rather than rely on an arguable exemption, the society
simply offers the 14 days. It costs almost nothing and removes the question.

## What to check when these are next updated

Each year, or whenever the membership changes:

- [ ] **Dates in 2.1** — they are specific to 2026–27 and will be wrong next year
- [ ] **Check 2.2 against the constitution**, particularly whether standing for
      committee is restricted to current University members
- [ ] **Contact address** still correct and still society-held, not personal
- [ ] Paste the whole updated text back into TryBooking's Global event settings
- [ ] Confirm the refund position in 4.3 still matches what the committee
      actually does in practice — terms that are not honoured are worse than none

See [TICKETING.md](TICKETING.md) for how ticketing works, and
[HANDOVER.md](HANDOVER.md) for the accounts these terms depend on.
