The Cambridge University Indian Classical Music Society exists to bring Indian
classical music — both Carnatic and Hindustani — to Cambridge, and to give
anyone in the University the chance to hear it, learn about it and play it.

We put on concerts by visiting artists, run workshops open to complete
beginners, and hold informal gatherings for members who play. No prior knowledge
is needed for anything we do, and our events are open to the public unless we say
otherwise.

## The two traditions

Indian classical music has two great living traditions. **Hindustani** music
developed in the north of the subcontinent, and **Carnatic** music in the south.
They share a common foundation — melody organised as _raga_, rhythm organised as
_tala_, and a central place for improvisation — but differ in repertoire,
instruments and performance practice.

The society programmes both, and does not treat either as the default.

## Getting involved

Anyone at the University can join, whatever their background and whether or not
they play. Come to a concert, come to a workshop, or write to us.
