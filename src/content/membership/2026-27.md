Dear Indian Classical Music enthusiasts,

We are pleased to share that membership of the Cambridge University Indian
Classical Music Society for 2026/27 is now open. If you would like to join us as
a member, you can sign up here:
<a href="https://forms.gle/nzr2xeUhQrtVVbR39" target="_blank" rel="noopener">forms.gle/nzr2xeUhQrtVVbR39</a>

## Annual rates

- Student Annual Membership: £12
- General Annual Membership: £15

## Member benefits

### Governance & Voice

- Voting rights at AGMs
- Eligibility to run for committee positions
- Input on society programming

### Priority Access

- Priority for performance opportunities in concerts
- Early access and some discounts on event tickets
- Priority booking for rehearsal spaces and instruments
- Access to funding for external music workshops
- Expense coverage when representing the Society

Our jamming sessions, concerts, events and this community remain open to
everyone, whether or not you are a member.

Please feel free to reach out to a member of the committee if you have any
questions.

Best wishes,<br>
The CU ICM Committee
