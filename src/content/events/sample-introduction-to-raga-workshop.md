What is a raga, and how is it different from a scale? This informal session
introduces the idea through listening and simple singing exercises. It is aimed
at people who have never encountered Indian classical music before, and assumes
no theoretical background.

Bring nothing but yourself. Everything is done by ear, and there is no
expectation that anyone sings alone.
