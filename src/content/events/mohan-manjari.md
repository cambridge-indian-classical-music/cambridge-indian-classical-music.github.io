The Cambridge University Indian Classical Music Society presents **Mohan
Manjari**, an evening devoted to Krishna, one of the most enduring and beloved
figures in India’s musical and devotional heritage.

For centuries, poets, saints and composers across India have sung of Krishna
in many guises: the mischievous child, the divine cowherd, the beloved of
Radha, and the teacher of the Bhagavad Gita. This concert brings together
Krishna-inspired compositions from across the subcontinent, including
**bhajans**, **kirtans** and **bandishes**, each shaped by the language, poetry
and musical traditions of its region.

The programme features performances in both the **Hindustani** and
**Carnatic** traditions, offering a rare opportunity to hear North and South
Indian classical music side by side, united by a single theme.
