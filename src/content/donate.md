The Cambridge University Indian Classical Music Society is run entirely by
volunteers. Ticket prices and membership fees keep our events affordable for
students, but they rarely cover the full cost of bringing artists to Cambridge.

Your donation helps us to:

- invite visiting artists in both the Carnatic and Hindustani traditions
- keep concert tickets affordable for students
- run workshops that are open to complete beginners
- look after the instruments our members play

Any amount makes a difference, and you can choose how much to give.
